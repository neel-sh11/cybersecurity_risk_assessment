2. Overview

This document presents an executive overview of the Information Security Risk Assessment conducted for Nexora Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

The assessment focuses on identifying information security risks, evaluating their potential impact, determining appropriate risk treatment measures, and reviewing the effectiveness of proposed security controls.

The project follows the principles of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 to demonstrate a structured approach to information security risk management.

⸻

3. Assessment Objectives

The primary objectives of this assessment are:

1. Identify potential information security risks.
2. Evaluate threats and vulnerabilities affecting organizational assets.
3. Determine risk levels using a consistent scoring methodology.
4. Identify appropriate security controls.
5. Develop a risk treatment plan.
6. Document control applicability and implementation status.
7. Evaluate remaining risks after treatment.

⸻

4. Key Risk Areas Identified

The assessment covers the following major information security risk areas:

4.1 Malware and Endpoint Security

Malware infections may compromise organizational systems, interrupt business operations, and expose sensitive information.

Recommended approach: Endpoint protection, security monitoring, and user awareness.

4.2 Data Protection and Availability

System failures or inadequate backup procedures may result in data loss and operational disruption.

Recommended approach: Regular backups, recovery testing, and appropriate data protection measures.

4.3 Access Management

Weak authentication or excessive user privileges may result in unauthorized access to organizational resources.

Recommended approach: Access restrictions, authentication controls, and periodic access reviews.

4.4 Vulnerability Management

Unpatched systems and technical vulnerabilities may expose organizational infrastructure to security threats.

Recommended approach: Vulnerability assessments, security updates, and patch management.

⸻

5. Risk Management Approach

The assessment follows a structured risk management process:

1. Asset identification and inventory.
2. Threat and vulnerability identification.
3. Risk analysis and evaluation.
4. Risk treatment planning.
5. Security control applicability assessment.
6. Implementation tracking.
7. Residual risk assessment.

Risks are evaluated using a likelihood and impact scoring model.

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

The resulting scores are categorized into Low, Moderate, High, and Critical risk levels.

⸻

6. Risk Treatment Strategy

The assessment considers four general risk treatment approaches:

* Risk Modification: Implementing security controls to reduce likelihood or impact.
* Risk Avoidance: Discontinuing activities that create unacceptable risks.
* Risk Sharing: Sharing certain risks through contractual arrangements or insurance.
* Risk Retention: Accepting risks through a documented and authorized decision.

Risk treatment decisions should be based on organizational requirements, available resources, and management approval.

⸻

7. Key Recommendations

Based on the identified risk areas, the following recommendations are proposed:

1. Establish and maintain an information security policy.
2. Implement role-based access control where appropriate.
3. Conduct regular vulnerability assessments.
4. Maintain reliable backup and recovery procedures.
5. Provide periodic security awareness training.
6. Establish an incident response procedure.
7. Monitor and review security controls regularly.
8. Maintain documented risk assessments and treatment decisions.

⸻

8. Expected Outcomes

The assessment is intended to support Nexora Solutions Pvt. Ltd. in:

* Improving awareness of information security risks.
* Establishing a structured risk management process.
* Prioritizing security improvements.
* Improving accountability for risk ownership.
* Supporting the development of an Information Security Management System (ISMS).
* Establishing a foundation for continual improvement.

⸻

9. Limitations

This project is a simulated GRC case study created for educational and portfolio purposes.

It does not represent an actual organizational audit, penetration test, certification assessment, or independently verified implementation of security controls.

Any control implementation status and residual risk conclusions must be supported by appropriate evidence before being treated as validated results.

⸻

10. Conclusion

Effective information security requires more than implementing technical controls. It requires structured risk assessment, management involvement, clear accountability, and continuous monitoring.

This assessment demonstrates a practical approach to identifying, evaluating, treating, and monitoring information security risks using ISO/IEC 27001:2022 as its primary reference framework.

The ultimate objective is to establish a risk-aware security management approach that supports organizational confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
