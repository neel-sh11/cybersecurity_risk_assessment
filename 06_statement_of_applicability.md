06 — Statement of Applicability (SoA)

Organisation: Nexora Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Framework: ISO/IEC 27001:2022
Reference: Annex A
Document Type: Preliminary Statement of Applicability
Document Status: Draft

⸻

1. Objective

The purpose of this Statement of Applicability (SoA) is to identify relevant information security controls for Nexora Solutions and document their applicability, implementation status and justification.

The SoA supports the organisation’s risk treatment process by connecting identified cybersecurity risks with appropriate ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls.

⸻

2. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A Structure

Annex A contains 93 reference controls divided into four categories.

A.5 — Organisational Controls

37 controls covering security policies, access management, supplier relationships and information security governance.

A.6 — People Controls

8 controls covering employee responsibilities, awareness, training and personnel-related security requirements.

A.7 — Physical Controls

14 controls covering physical security, equipment protection and secure working environments.

A.8 — Technological Controls

34 controls covering authentication, network security, malware protection, backup and technical vulnerability management.

Total: 93 controls

⸻

3. Applicability Criteria

Controls are evaluated using the following considerations:

1. Identified cybersecurity risks.
2. Business operations and information assets.
3. Legal, regulatory and contractual requirements.
4. Organisational security objectives.
5. Applicable risk treatment decisions.

A control is considered applicable when it is necessary to address relevant security requirements or risks.

An excluded control must have a documented justification.

⸻

6. Implementation Status Definitions

The following status definitions will be used in future SoA reviews:

Not Implemented: The required control has not been implemented.

Partially Implemented: Some elements of the control are implemented, but gaps remain.

Implemented: The control has been implemented and supporting evidence is available.

Not Verified: Implementation status has not yet been established through assessment evidence.

For this case study, the selected controls are proposed recommendations and have not been verified as implemented.

⸻

7. Exclusions and Limitations

This document represents a preliminary selection of 18 controls.

It is not a complete Statement of Applicability for ISO/IEC 27001 certification purposes.

A complete SoA must consider all 93 Annex A controls, document applicability decisions, justify exclusions and record implementation status.

Physical facility security and additional organisational requirements require further assessment.

8. Assessment Conclusion

It provides a structured foundation for control implementation tracking, evidence collection and future risk reassessment.

The next stage will focus on tracking the proposed controls, assigning responsibilities and documenting implementation evidence.
